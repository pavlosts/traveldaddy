# Encounter

Felt spec for the prototype. Write the scene first. Do not put hex codes, radii, or component tokens in this file.

## The scene

Sunday night. Couch, phone brightness high. Suitcase half-open for a Friday flight. They open Instagram saves, then TikTok favorites, then a Notes dump of screenshots. Same cafe tip appears twice; half the clips are places they cannot name. They put the phone down, jaw tight, and still do not know what to book tomorrow.

## What it does to you

- Breathing: Exhale when the trip finally becomes a short place page instead of a scroll spiral.
- Warm or cool: Warm travel-daylight, not cold productivity SaaS.
- After you close the tab: Quiet confidence: "I already had the answers; now I can see them."

## Lenses

- Taste / scent: Street coffee, sunscreen stick, boarding-pass paper.
- Weather: Late-afternoon gold before a trip week.
- Material: Soft paper map meets phone glass; clipped stills from Reels, not stock tourism posters.
- Emotional state: From scattered FOMO to oriented and ready.
- Social signal (who this is for): The friend who always "knows a spot" because they save everything.
- Life season: Pre-trip crunch or rainy-day someday planning.

## Pairings (keep both)

Oriented without corporate; playful without silly; dense with tips without feeling like a spreadsheet.

## Not this

- Generic AI chatbot with a globe emoji
- Booking.com clone
- Infinite vertical feed that recreates the save pile
- Purple SaaS dashboard vibes

## Prototype rule

Generate from the scene. If it matches tokens but lost the scene, reject it. Extract the system only after it still tastes right.
