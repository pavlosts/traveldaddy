# Happy customer letter

Fake letter from a real customer to the CEO. Write it **before** design or build. Share it with the team so everyone feels **why** this matters, not just what to ship.

If you cannot write a credible letter, the problem framing is not ready.

## Letter (customer voice, as if it already worked)

Dear Travel Daddy team,

I am the person who saves every Athens food Reel and Lisbon viewpoint Short, then panics the week before the trip. Before you, I spent two nights rewatching my Instagram and TikTok saves, screenshots everywhere, still unsure what was even in the same neighborhood.

I dropped my saves into Travel Daddy. You grouped them by place and gave me one page I could actually scan: food, spots, little tips, all tied to where I was going. I planned Saturday in Lisbon from that page in under twenty minutes instead of another doom-scroll.

I wrote because the relief was physical. I stopped treating my saves like a junk drawer and started treating them like a trip I could use.

Thank you,

Maya, weekend-trip saver

## Filter

Every design and engineering choice should make this letter truer. If the letter sounds forced, rework problem framing before you build.
