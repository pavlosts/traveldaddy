# Users

Hackathon short. One primary person. Not a research-validated persona.

## Primary

- Name (working): Maya
- Role: Travel-curious social saver (consumer)
- Job: Capture trip ideas while scrolling; later turn them into a usable plan for a place
- Buyer vs user: Same person (consumer app)
- Use environment: Phone on couch or in bed at night; split attention; Wi‑Fi; often pre-trip week. Sometimes desktop when synthesizing the itinerary.
- Past behavior (claimed, unvalidated): Saves Reels/Shorts across Instagram and TikTok; sometimes screenshots into Notes or pins on Maps; reopens saves when planning.

## What they hope / fear

- Hope: Open one place page and see what they already saved, ready to act.
- Fear: Missing a great tip buried in the pile; wasting the night before the trip.

## Alternatives they already use

Instagram saves, TikTok favorites, Notes screenshots, Google Maps lists, asking friends.
