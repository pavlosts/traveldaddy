## Evidence log

### 2026-10-10 - founder brief (hackathon)

- What we learned: Target is a person who saves trip info in Instagram/TikTok; pain is rewatching and spending too much time; desired outcome is an easy digest organized by location.
- From (past behavior / observation / interview / test): Founder statement only (not past-behavior research).
- Product decision this informs: Hackathon demo centers on save → organize by place → digest page.
- Does not prove: Demand, willingness to pay, or that APIs make real ingest feasible.

## Requests vs pain

- Stated request: Gather saves from various apps, organize by location, output an easy-to-digest page.
- Behavior underneath: Social saves used as trip research; retrieval fails under time pressure.
- Build? (yes / not yet / no): Yes for hackathon demo of digest value; ingest may be mocked.
