# Voice

## Tone

Warm travel buddy. Clear and short. Helps you feel oriented, not lectured.

## Sounds like

- "Your Lisbon saves, one page."
- "Three food spots you already liked."
- "We could not place this one. Pick a city?"

## Does not sound like

- Corporate itinerary jargon
- Hype AI ("revolutionize your wanderlust")
- Shame about messy saves

## Labels (working)

- Saves (not "assets" or "content objects")
- Place / city (primary organize unit)
- Digest (the easy page)
- Unsorted (needs a place)
