# Problem

What this product is solving. Fill from discovery. Do not invent a problem to justify a feature.

## Who

- Role: Casual trip planner / travel-curious saver (consumer)
- Context: Scrolls Instagram Reels and TikTok Shorts; saves places, food, tips, and "someday" spots across apps
- Situation: Planning a trip soon, or banked saves for a future trip, and cannot remember what they saved or where it was about

Could someone pick this person out of a crowd? Mostly yes: the social-native saver who treats Reels/Shorts as a travel notebook.

## The pain

- What hurts: Useful trip info is trapped in a long, messy save pile across apps. Rewatching to find "that Athens food tip" burns time and attention.
- How they cope today: Reopen Instagram/TikTok saves, scroll, rewatch, screenshot into Notes/Maps, or give up and Google again.
- Cost of doing nothing: Hours lost before the trip; forgotten gems; half-baked itineraries; decision fatigue.
- Frequency: Heavy before trips; light ongoing when they save "for later."
- Why this is THE priority (not A problem): The job is not "another bookmark app." It is turning scattered social saves into a place-based digest they can actually use.

## Happy customer (one sentence)

A Reel-and-Shorts saver opens Travel Daddy for a destination and gets a clear, location-organized digest of what they already saved, without rewatching the pile.

## Alternatives

- Do nothing: Keep scrolling saves when trip anxiety hits.
- Workarounds: Screenshots, Notes, Google Maps lists, Notion boards, shared Google Docs.
- Direct rivals: Generic bookmark tools, trip planners that ignore social saves, "AI itinerary" apps that start from blank prompts instead of your saves.

## Community to product

- Community: Travel-curious social scrollers who already save Reels/Shorts as research.
- Problem they already have: Saves pile up; retrieval and sense-making fail when it is time to plan.
- Product that follows: Travel Daddy gathers those saves, organizes by location, outputs an easy digest.
- Care + willingness to pay (rough): Unvalidated. Hackathon bet: free demo first; pay later if digest quality is sticky.

## Not this

- Feature idea, competitor parity, or preferred solution with no observed pain: Building a full social network, creator tools, or generic AI chat without the save-to-digest loop.
